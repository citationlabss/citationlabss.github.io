Altmetrics

Beyond citations, we investigate alternative signals of research attention and engagement — social media mentions, news coverage, policy citations, and online discussion — and how these relate to traditional impact measures.

Citation Context Analysis

We examine the textual context surrounding citations to understand why authors cite the work they do — distinguishing supportive, contrasting, and background references, and what this reveals about scholarly influence.

Lab

Citation Inequality / Concentration / Skewness

Citations are distributed unevenly across papers, authors, and institutions. We study the shape of this inequality — concentration among a small share of highly cited work — and its implications for how science is evaluated.
